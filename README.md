# Campus Career Fair & Application Portal

A centralized platform designed to streamline the career fair experience for students and recruiters at PennWest California. This system bridges the gap between attending a job and org fair and landing an interview by combining instant QR code applications, targeted candidate filtering, and direct post-fair communication.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Attending career fairs often leaves students navigating a fragmented job hunt—collecting flyers, applying separately through external company portals, and rarely hearing back. Employers, on the other hand, struggle to track candidates met on-site or filter through hundreds of general applicants.

This project solves these issues by unifying job fair management with an integrated job application system. Students can instantly apply to participating companies during a fair by scanning a table-specific **QR Code**, while companies gain immediate access to student profiles, resumes, and post-event messaging tools.

---

## ⚠️ Existing Problems Solved

* **Post-Fair Friction:** Eliminates the need for students to re-apply on third-party company career sites after meeting recruiters in person.
* **Lost Connection Context:** Keeps student applications tied directly to the specific job fair event they attended.
* **Recruiter Overload:** Helps companies filter for specific majors, skills, and certifications directly during or after the event.
* **Fragmented Information:** Replaces scattered job postings and external links with a single, centralized university fair hub.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### For Students
* **Account & Profile Creation:** Secure authentication with profile customization.
* **Comprehensive Portfolio:** Display academic background, major, work experience, certifications, career interests, and uploaded resume.
* **Job Discovery:** Browse active job listings with tailored filter options.
* **Instant QR Application:** Scan company table QR codes on-site at career fairs to submit applications on the spot.
* **Direct Messaging:** Communicate directly with employers regarding updates, questions, or interview invitations post-fair.

### For Companies
* **Event Registration:** Register to participate in upcoming campus career fairs.
* **Job Posting Management:** Create detailed job listings specifying requirements and ideal candidate profiles.
* **On-Site QR Generation:** Receive a unique, table-ready QR code upon fair registration for instant student application scanning.
* **Talent Search & Filtering:** Search through PennWest California student profiles based on major, skills, and qualifications.
* **Application Review & Outreach:** Review incoming applications, select top candidates, and schedule interviews within the platform.
